# Parametric three-T two-CNOT threshold and symplectic mechanism

**Research date:** 29 October 2026. **Theorem status:** exact finite certificate + independent state-vector checks. **Literature priority:** not established. This is an extension of the [single-state exact three-T witness](three_t_two_cnot_threshold.md), not an independent experimental paper.

## A rigorous interval of *intrinsically magical* inputs

For every real s with -1/8 <= s <= 1/4 define the normalized pure two-qubit state
\[
|\psi(s)\rangle=
\frac{(-1+3i)|00\rangle+(-7-i)|01\rangle+(-1+i)|10\rangle+[-1+(4+s)i]|11\rangle}
{\sqrt{N(s)}},\quad N(s)=79+8s+s^2.
\]
With H/S single-qubit Cliffords, at most three local T gates, no ancillas or measurements, and CNOT gate-count budget d, let
\(R_d(\psi)=\min_\mathrm{admissible\,circuit}\sum_{P\in\{I,X,Y,Z\}^{\otimes2}}\langle P\rangle^4\).
The unrestricted at-most-three-T circuit family is attained using at most **two** CNOTs (see [two-CNOT constructive compiler](../src/three_t_two_cnot_compiler.py)).

**NEW parametric theorem (uniform strict gap):**
\[
\boxed{\quad
R_{\le1\mathrm{CNOT}}(\psi(s))-R_{\le2\mathrm{CNOT}}(\psi(s))
>\frac{1}{100}\quad\forall s\in[-1/8,1/4].
\quad}
\]
Consequently, for **every** state on this continuous family, **exactly two CNOTs are necessary and sufficient** to reach the maximum output M2 permitted by at most three T gates.

We do not need to identify the unrestricted optimal word at every s. Instead, a **single fixed 2-CNOT witness**, chronological Pauli rotations \((YY,XI,XY)\), beats every one-CNOT-or-less competitor by at least 1/100, which implies the inequality because the unrestricted optimal moment cannot exceed the witness moment.

The states remain partially entangled with unavoidable local-unitary magic:
\[
C(s)^2=\frac{4(10s^2+116s+362)}{(79+8s+s^2)^2}\in(0,1)
\]
on the entire interval. This range includes s=0 (the original exact isolated witness), but extends substantially beyond the previous tiny continuity ball.

## Complete exact polynomial certificate, not interval sampling

For each of the 16 Pauli strings P, the **unnormalized** expectation of P in the Gaussian-integer vector above is a degree-2 polynomial of s with integer coefficients:
\[
m_P(s)=\langle v(s)|P|v(s)\rangle\in\mathbb Z[s]_{\le2}.
\]
Conjugation by each Pauli T rotates pairs of Pauli expectations by 45 degrees. For any signed at-most-three-T word W, the normalized fourth moment is
\[
R_W(\psi(s))=\frac{F_W(s)}{(8N(s))^4},
\qquad F_W(s)\in\mathbb Z[\sqrt2][s]_{\le8},
\]
after scaling shorter words to the same positive denominator.

The code [src/three_t_parametric_certificate.py](../src/three_t_parametric_certificate.py) compares the fixed 2-CNOT witness W=(YY,XI,XY) against an **overcomplete** conservative class of every zero/one-CNOT circuit with at most three T:
- 1 + 15 + 225 = **241** unrestricted zero/one/two-T words, including circuits potentially requiring >1 CNOT (safe overapproximation);
- all **2457** ordered Pauli triples generated by local Clifford axis permutations and **one CNOT at any of four positions**, independently of which T gates are inverted;
- all **eight** T/T-dagger sign patterns for each triple.

Total comparisons: \(241+2457\cdot8=19\,897\).

For each competitor V, let \(D_V(s)=F_V(s)-F_W(s)\). Substitute \(s=(3t-1)/8\) to map [-1/8,1/4] onto \(t\in[0,1]\), and expand
\[
35\cdot8^8 D_V((3t-1)/8)
=\sum_{j=0}^{8}b_{V,j}\binom8j t^j(1-t)^{8-j}.
\]
All **179,073** exact Bernstein coefficients \(b_{V,j}\in\mathbb Z[\sqrt2]\) satisfy a stronger, entirely integer-checkable bound:
\[
\boxed{1600\,b_{V,j}>
35\cdot8^8\cdot1297^4,\quad\forall V,j.}
\]
Here \(N(s)\le N(1/4)=1297/16\), so \((8N(s))^4\le1297^4/16\). By positivity and convex partition of unity of the Bernstein basis, the displayed bound immediately implies
\[
R_V(\psi(s))-R_W(\psi(s))>1/100\quad\forall s\in[-1/8,1/4].
\]
Signs of \(a+b\sqrt2\) are proved with integer comparisons of \(a^2\) versus \(2b^2\), *not* by floating point. Independent dense state-vector tests at five values of s confirm the direct T-gate evolution and the polynomial fourth moments.

This is a **computer-assisted exact proof**, conditional on correctness of the exhaustive one-CNOT circuit normal-form classification; the latter is separately documented and tested.

## Why three T is structurally different: an elementary symplectic classification

Identify each sign-free two-qubit Pauli with a nonzero vector of \(\mathbb F_2^4\), with commutation encoded by the symplectic bilinear form
\[
[p,q]=x_0 z'_0+z_0 x'_0+x_1 z'_1+z_1 x'_1\pmod2.
\]
For an ordered triple (P,Q,R), define its binary span rank r and graph E of pairwise **anticommutation** edges.

**Localization lemma:** Three Pauli axes can be simultaneously Clifford-conjugated to weight-one axes **if and only if** neither condition occurs:
1. r=3 and E has at least two edges;
2. r=2, all three axes are distinct and commute pairwise.

**Proof sketch:** Weight-one Paulis anticommute only when supported on the same qubit. A connected three-node anticommutation graph forces all three axes onto one qubit, giving binary rank <=2; hence (1) is impossible. Three distinct commuting nonidentity weight-one Paulis cannot coexist on two qubits (at most one distinct choice per qubit), proving (2). Conversely, all remaining rank/commutation cases have explicit weight-one representatives (rank1: XI; rank2 symplectic: XI,ZI,YI; rank2 commuting with repetition: XI,IX; rank3 with one anticommutation pair: XI,ZI,IX), and a symplectic isometry between spans extends to a Clifford.

An exact \(\mathbb F_2\) enumeration [src/three_t_symplectic_obstruction.py](../src/three_t_symplectic_obstruction.py) yields:
| Triple type | Total | Cannot simultaneously localize |
|---|---:|---:|
| Rank 1 | 15 | 0 |
| Rank 2, zero anti edges | 360 | 90 |
| Rank 2, two anti edges | 360 | 0 |
| Rank 2, three anti edges | 120 | 0 |
| Rank 3, one anti edge | 1080 | 0 |
| Rank 3, two anti edges | 1080 | 1080 |
| Rank 3, three anti edges | 360 | 360 |
| **Total** | **3375** | **1530** |

However, **612** of those 1530 nonlocalizable triples can still be implemented with one **interleaved** CNOT, since a single pre-Clifford is not the only possible circuit structure. The complete <=1-CNOT signed-axis enumeration has 2457 accessible distinct triples; 918 are inaccessible as ordered triple representations. The witness word **(YY,XI,XY)** has rank3 and two anticommuting edges, and is not in the accessible set.

These structural obstructions are **necessary, not sufficient**, for a strict magic-gain separation; another one-CNOT word could conceivably reach the same output magic. The 19,897-candidate exact certificate is what excludes that possibility for the interval family.

The theorem concerns *physical CNOT count*, not fully timed hardware circuit depth or noisy implementations. No ancillas or measurements. It does not classify every input state for which two CNOTs are necessary.

**Literature caution:** Zhu et al. (2024) proved universal amortized one-T bounds; Bian and Selinger (2023) established Clifford+T Pauli normal forms; Webster et al. (2026) optimize general Clifford/CNOT synthesis; Hou et al. (2026) analyze magic injection including nonstabilizer inputs. General commuting-Pauli/Clifford diagonalization techniques are also prior art (e.g. Gokhale et al. and subsequent graph-based diagonalization literature). Our elementary symplectic rank classification should not be marketed as new stabilizer theory. The **input-conditioned strict CNOT resource separation over a parametric magical family** is the possible new contribution, **not yet verified as publication-novel**.
